## Austin & San Antonio: From Secondary Markets to Powerhouse Hub

The Austin-San Antonio corridor has rapidly transformed into one of America's most dynamic data center regions. What industry insiders once considered "secondary markets" have surged to prominence, capitalizing on Texas's business-friendly environment and the explosive demand for cloud and AI infrastructure.

### Market Growth Metrics That Matter

- **Capacity Explosion:** The combined Austin-San Antonio market quadrupled in size since 2020
- **Under Development:** 463.5 MW of new capacity currently under development
- **Market Position:** Now ranks as the second-largest data center market in the U.S. after Northern Virginia
- **Vacancy Rate:** Critically low ~4% vacancy, indicating severe supply constraints
- **Current Capacity:** 646 MW built, with another 664 MW under construction
- **Future Pipeline:** 1,229 MW additional capacity planned
- **Leasing Velocity:** New facilities are being leased almost as fast as they can be built

As JLL's data center expert Curt Holcomb notes, "We're seeing really insatiable growth," driven primarily by surging cloud services and artificial intelligence workloads. The same AI revolution powering technologies like ChatGPT is simultaneously creating massive demand for server infrastructure.

### Real-World Development Examples

This explosive growth is visible across the region:

- **Microsoft's Castroville Campus:** Converting farmland west of San Antonio into massive data center facilities
- **San Marcos Corridor:** New data centers rising along rural backroads between Austin and San Antonio
- **Williamson County:** Significant development north of Austin bringing hundreds of megawatts online
- **Bastrop Expansion:** Eastern growth corridor attracting major data center investments

## Waco and the I-35 Corridor: Tomorrow's Data Center Frontier

While Austin and San Antonio lead the current boom, the next wave of development is already emerging along the I-35 corridor toward Waco and Temple. This region offers compelling advantages for hyperscale operators seeking large-scale development opportunities.

### Why Waco Is Gaining Momentum

- **Land Availability:** Abundant, affordable tracts suitable for 500+ acre hyperscale campuses
- **Strategic Location:** Positioned along the I-35 corridor between major Texas metros
- **Disaster Recovery:** Equidistant from Dallas, Austin, and Houston, ideal for backup facilities
- **Cost Advantages:** Significantly lower land costs than established markets

### Spotlight: Meta's $800 Million Temple Investment

Meta (Facebook's parent company) exemplifies this trend with its [$800 million hyperscale project in Temple](https://www.kwtx.com/2022/03/31/facebook-parent-company-invest-800-million-central-texas/), just outside Waco. This massive development:

- Spans 900,000+ square feet across nearly 400 acres
- Features dual power substations and robust fiber connectivity
- Will create thousands of construction jobs
- Is designed for multi-phase expansion over the next decade

### Edge Computing Applications

Waco's central location makes it ideal for edge computing applications, with companies like [DataBank operating facilities](https://www.databank.com/data-centers/waco/waco-700-austin-avenue/) that provide:

- Disaster recovery services for major Texas cities
- Low-latency backup capabilities
- Regional content delivery networks
- Support for IoT and autonomous vehicle testing

## 5 Factors Driving Central Texas's Data Center Advantage

Several key factors converge to make Central Texas uniquely positioned for continued data center growth:

### 1. Strategic Land Resources

- **Abundant Space:** Wide-open spaces along corridors like I-35 offer room for expansion
- **Scale Potential:** 100+ acre sites that would be impossible to find in Silicon Valley or downtown Dallas
- **Affordability:** Land costs remain competitive, especially for large campuses
- **Disaster Resistance:** Inland location reduces hurricane and earthquake risks versus coastal regions

### 2. Power Infrastructure Advantages

- **Capacity:** Texas's deregulated ERCOT grid can rapidly scale generation capacity
- **Cost Efficiency:** Electricity costs rank among the nation's lowest
- **Energy Diversity:** Mix of natural gas, wind, and solar generation
- **Renewable Leadership:** Texas leads the U.S. in wind power production
- **Resilience Investments:** Many new facilities include on-site generation and battery storage

### 3. Business-Friendly Climate & Incentives

- **Tax Advantages:** No state corporate income tax
- **Data Center Exemptions:** [Texas Data Center Exemption program](https://www.datacenters.com/news/from-cattle-ranches-to-server-farms-texas-s-rural-data-boom) offers sales tax breaks on equipment and infrastructure
- **Local Incentives:** County and city property tax abatements and expedited permitting
- **Cost Comparison:** Operating costs significantly lower than California or Northeast markets

### 4. Connectivity Infrastructure

- **Fiber Network:** Extensive investment in fiber optic networks throughout the region
- **Backbone Access:** Direct connections to major Internet backbones
- **Rural Expansion:** Multi-gigabit connectivity reaching previously underserved areas
- **Low Latency:** Fast connections between major Texas metros

### 5. Tech Ecosystem & Talent Pipeline

- **Talent Migration:** Texas ranks #1 for tech worker migration nationally
- **Skills Development:** Partnerships with local colleges to train specialized data center technicians
- **Tech Synergies:** Proximity to semiconductor manufacturing, AI startups, and technology innovators
- **Contractor Network:** Established ecosystem of specialized data center contractors and suppliers
