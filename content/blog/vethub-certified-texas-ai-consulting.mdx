# It's Official — Texas AI Consulting Is VetHUB Certified

**March 12, 2026 — a date I'll never forget.**

Today, I received the letter from the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts. Texas AI Consulting, LLC is officially **VetHUB certified** by the State of Texas.

I sat with that letter for a while before I could even process it. Not because it was unexpected — I'd been working toward it since the day I filed my LLC. But because of what it *means*. What it represents. And the road it took to get here.

---

## A Lifetime of Service

I've spent my entire adult life in service. That's not something I say lightly — it's just the truth.

At seventeen years old, I raised my right hand and swore an oath to defend this country. I served **21 years** in the United States Air Force Reserve and the Air National Guard. I deployed to **Iraq** and **Afghanistan** — three combat deployments total. I flew aeromedical evacuation missions as a **Flight Nurse** with the 459th Aeromedical Evacuation Squadron, caring for wounded warriors inside cargo aircraft at altitude, with limited equipment, making life-or-death decisions that couldn't wait.

For that service, I was awarded the **Air Force Air Medal**.

While I wore the uniform, I also maintained a full-time civilian nursing career — twenty years at the bedside, caring for families in their most vulnerable moments. Emergency departments. ICUs. Progressive care units. I worked in the places most people never want to see — and I did it willingly, because that's who I am.

**Service is not something I did. It's something I am.**

---

## Why This Moment Hits Different

When I founded Texas AI Consulting on January 28, 2026, I wasn't just starting a business. I was answering a call. The same kind of call I answered when I enlisted. The same kind of call I answered every time I walked into a patient's room.

This time, the call came from Texas.

The State of Texas created the **VetHUB (Veteran Historically Underutilized Business)** program to recognize veteran-owned businesses and give them a seat at the table for state contracting opportunities. Texas recently restructured the entire HUB program, significantly tightening eligibility requirements. Being certified means something real — it means you've been vetted, validated, and recognized by the great State of Texas.

And now, **Texas AI Consulting holds that certification.**

That means everything to a veteran.

## Thank You, Texas

I want to say something from my heart: **thank you, Texas.**

Thank you for creating a program that honors the men and women who served this nation. Thank you for giving veterans a real pathway — not just lip service, but an actual seat at the table. Thank you for recognizing that the discipline, the resilience, the leadership, and the sheer grit that comes from military service translates directly into business excellence.

And as someone who chose to make Texas home after years of service across this country and around the world, I'm deeply grateful.

**I served my country for 21 years.** Across oceans. In combat zones. In hospitals. In the air and on the ground.

Texas AI Consulting isn't just a business to me. It's the culmination of everything I've been building toward. A veteran-owned, Texas-certified company that brings clinical expertise, technical excellence, and a servant's heart to every project we touch.
